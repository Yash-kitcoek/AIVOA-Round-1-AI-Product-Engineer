I am writing to formally notify you of a significant product quality complaint received from one of our hospital clients, City General Hospital, Mumbai.

COMPLAINT DETAILS:
Product: Metformin HCl 500mg Tablets (Immediate Release)
Batch Number: MT-2026-0342
Manufacturing Date: March 2026
Expiry Date: February 2029
Quantity Affected: 200 blister packs (10 x 10)

DESCRIPTION OF COMPLAINT:
Our pharmacist at City General Hospital reported that during routine in-house quality verification testing, the dissolution profile of the above batch deviated significantly from the labeled specification. The dissolution at 45 minutes was measured at approximately 52%, whereas the specification requires NLT 80% (Q).

The pharmacist also noted visible cracks along the tablet surface in approximately 15% of the sampled tablets, which may indicate compression issues during manufacturing.

IMPACT:
Metformin is a critical first-line treatment for Type 2 Diabetes. Subtherapeutic dissolution may result in inadequate blood glucose control in diabetic patients, potentially leading to hyperglycemia and associated complications.
