Product: Metformin HCl 500mg Tablets (Immediate Release)

Quantity Affected (estimated): 3,200 units (16%)
Complaint Source: Hospital quality department

COMPLAINT DETAILS:
Our hospital client, City General Hospital (Bengaluru), flagged this batch after conducting
routine dissolution testing per their incoming quality checks as per their QMS protocol.

The dissolution results are as follows:
- USP Apparatus II, 75 RPM, 900 mL phosphate buffer pH 6.8, 45 minutes
- Specification: NLT 80% (Q)
- Observed Result: ~52% dissolved at 45 minutes (OOS — Out of Specification)

In addition to the dissolution failure, their pharmacist also observed physical defects:
- Visible surface cracks on approximately 15% of tablets examined
- Slight discolouration (yellowish tinge) on 5% of tablets

CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Metformin HCl is a first-line oral antidiabetic medication critical for Type 2 Diabetes
management. A dissolution failure of this magnitude (52% vs NLT 80%) means significantly
subtherapeutic drug exposure in patients, which may result in:
- Inadequate blood glucose control
- Risk of hyperglycaemic episodes
- Potential for long-term diabetic complications

ACTIONS ALREADY TAKEN BY OUR HOSPITAL CLIENT:
1. Entire Batch MT-2026-0342 stock quarantined at City General Hospital
2. Alternate supplier stock arranged to ensure continuity of patient care
3. Incident logged in their pharmacovigilance system

OUR REQUEST:
1. Acknowledge receipt of this complaint and provide a formal complaint number within 24 hours
2. Investigate root cause — potential areas: granulation parameters, binder concentration,
   compression force settings, or raw material variability
3. Conduct testing of retained reference samples from Batch MT-2026-0342
4. Confirm whether other distributed batches may be impacted
5. Initiate market recall if investigation confirms systemic failure
6. Provide written CAPA response within 10 working days

We would appreciate your urgent attention to this matter given the direct patient safety implications.
