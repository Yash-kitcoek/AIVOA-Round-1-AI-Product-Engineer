COMPLAINT DESCRIPTION:
During dispensing, our pharmacist at the Hyderabad branch noticed that the capsules from Batch AMX-2026-0198 appeared to have a distinctly different color than the standard product — the capsule body was light pink instead of the expected white.

Upon further investigation across 3 additional boxes from the same batch purchased at our Pune and Bangalore branches, we identified the same anomaly. The capsule contents appeared brownish-orange rather than white/off-white powder, suggesting possible cross-contamination with another product.

We sent 5 capsules to our empaneled external laboratory (Analytical Labs India, Hyderabad) for rapid testing. The preliminary HPLC analysis showed the presence of what appears to be a secondary compound peak that should not be present in a pure Amoxicillin formulation.

SEVERITY ASSESSMENT: CRITICAL
This is a potential cross-contamination incident. Amoxicillin is a beta-lactam antibiotic. Any contamination with non-beta-lactam compounds (or vice versa) poses a serious patient safety risk, including potential allergic reactions and therapeutic failure.

ACTIONS TAKEN BY REPORTER:
- All stock of Batch AMX-2026-0198 has been quarantined at all Apollo branches.
- Approximately 2,400 boxes are held pending your investigation outcome.
- External lab test report is available upon request.
- No adverse patient outcomes have been reported to date, but we are monitoring.

REGULATORY NOTE:
Given the nature of this complaint, Apollo Pharmacy management believes this may require regulatory reporting to CDSCO under Schedule M compliance. We request your confirmation within 48 hours.
