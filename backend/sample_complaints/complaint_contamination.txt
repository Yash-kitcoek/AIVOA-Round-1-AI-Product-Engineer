RE: POSSIBLE CROSS-CONTAMINATION – Amoxicillin Trihydrate Capsules 500mg – Batch AMX-2026-0198

Dear Quality Assurance Team,

We are writing to formally report a serious quality concern that was identified at one of our
central distribution warehouses during routine pre-dispatch inspection.

COMPLAINT DETAILS:
Product Name: Amoxicillin Trihydrate Capsules 500mg
Batch Number: AMX-2026-0198
Manufacturing Date: May 2026
Expiry Date: April 2028
Quantity Affected: Approximately 500 capsule packs (250 units)
Complaint Source: Pharmacy chain QA inspection

INCIDENT DESCRIPTION:
During dispensing, our pharmacist at HealthPlus Branch 14, Mumbai, noticed that capsules
from this batch had an unusual colour — light pink instead of the expected opaque white
that is standard for Amoxicillin Trihydrate. Further, the powder inside the capsules appeared
brownish-orange rather than the characteristic white/off-white colour.

We immediately isolated all affected stock from Batch AMX-2026-0198 and sent samples to
an external accredited laboratory for analysis.

LABORATORY FINDINGS (Preliminary):
External HPLC analysis performed at ClinLabs India Pvt. Ltd. (NABL Accredited, Lab No. 2287)
showed the presence of a secondary compound peak at a retention time that does NOT correspond
to Amoxicillin Trihydrate, suggesting possible cross-contamination with another active
pharmaceutical ingredient during manufacturing.

SEVERITY ASSESSMENT:
This is a CRITICAL quality and patient safety event. Amoxicillin is a beta-lactam antibiotic.
Any cross-contamination with another API presents serious patient safety risks:
- Potential allergic reactions if the contaminant is from a different drug class
- Inadequate therapeutic effect due to reduced Amoxicillin potency
- Risk of medication error if the visual appearance change goes unnoticed by end pharmacists

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS TAKEN BY APOLLO PHARMACY CHAIN:
1. All stock of Batch AMX-2026-0198 quarantined across all 46 distribution points (National)
2. Regulatory authority (CDSCO) and state drug controller notified (Complaint Ref: MH-ADR-2026-874)
3. Investigation opened internally under reference APC-QA-2026-0720
4. Prescribing physicians for all patients who received this batch being contacted

FORMAL REQUEST:
1. Immediate acknowledgment and assignment of complaint number
2. Batch retention sample testing by your internal QC and QA teams
3. Root cause investigation including equipment cleaning validation and campaign manufacturing logs
4. Determination if any other batches are potentially affected
5. Field alert or voluntary recall initiation if systemic failure confirmed
6. Written investigation report within 7 working days

This matter requires immediate escalation to your Quality Director. We are available for a
conference call at your earliest convenience.
